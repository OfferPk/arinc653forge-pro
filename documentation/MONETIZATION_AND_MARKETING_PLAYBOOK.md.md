# ARINC653Forge Pro Monetization & Marketing Playbook

## 1\. Commercial Market Landscape

ARINC 653 is the gold standard for safety-critical real-time operating systems in civil and military aviation (Boeing 787, Airbus A350, KC-46, Eurofighter). Traditional commercial RTOS licenses (Wind River VxWorks 653, SYSGO PikeOS, Green Hills INTEGRITY-178) cost \$30,000 to \$75,000 per developer seat. ARINC653Forge Pro delivers accessible, browser-native partition scheduling and APEX verification to avionics systems architects and DO-178C software engineering teams.
